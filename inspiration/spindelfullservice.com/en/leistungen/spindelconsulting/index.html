WITH HIGHEST KNOW-HOW for Fritz Studer AG

Precision of the entire machine massively improved

Initial situation:
M
The company used a GMN TSE 170c-7.500/10.5 grinding spindle for its grinding machine. This should have been a high-precision machine with perfect concentricity and a vibration value of less than 0.2 mm/s. However, this was not the case. However, this was not the case.
Procedure:
N
After inspection and disassembly, we found that the bearing seats of the shaft were clearly worn.
N
With our innovative coating process, we have completely refurbished the shaft and thus massively extended its service life.
N
To prevent temperature problems at high speeds, we installed hybrid ceramic bearings at the rear with Kronidur steel.
Result:
N

Following the repair, the spindle is now significantly more wear-resistant and can therefore achieve its full performance potential. The failure rate has been greatly reduced, while the quality of the sanding work has improved significantly thanks to perfect concentricity and vibration values below 0.2 mm/s.

MODIFICATION OF ALL WHITE ENGINE SPINDLES for BMW

Reduction of the failure rate by more than 50 %

Initial situation:
M
BMW was struggling with massive spindle failures that regularly caused major delays in production. The company turned to us to solve this problem and avoid having to keep buying new spindles.
Procedure:
N
With our failure analysis, we were able to determine exactly which components were repeatedly causing problems. This enabled us to make some special modifications and massively increase the quality of the spindle.
Result:
N
With our optimizations, we were able to reduce the failure rate by over 50 % and greatly reduce the overall costs for BMW. At the same time, the company is no longer dependent on manufacturers and has modified spindles that run even better and more precisely than new ones would have done.
